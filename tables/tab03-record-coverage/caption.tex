\textbf{Table 3: Resource coverage of the manuscript record types.}\space
\figpanel{a}{Coverage of 18 manuscript record types across the integrated OmniPath Metabo resources. A checkmark indicates that the resource contributes the record type. Most cells come from cycle-001 bitmap-intersection queries against \texttt{facet\_entity\_bitmap} (\texttt{entity\_type}, \texttt{chemical\_class}, \texttt{ontology\_id}, \texttt{structural\_specificity}) and \texttt{facet\_relation\_bitmap} (\texttt{predicate}); the Structures row is the only one that routes to \texttt{dev4} (\texttt{structural\_specificity} facet bitmap is dev4-only). A small number of cells come from curator domain knowledge that the cycle-001 \texttt{vocab\_relation\_predicate.interaction\_class\_id} map cannot yet express: the build assigns predicates to \textit{Signaling}, \textit{Transport}, and \textit{Other} only, so resources whose distinctive contribution is \textit{Allosteric regulation} (BRENDA), \textit{TF-target} (SIGNOR), \textit{Ligand-receptor} (CellChat, CellinKer, CellPhoneDB, ConnectomeDB, Guide to Pharmacology, ICellNet, MEBOCOST, MRCLinks, NeuronChat, NicheNet), \textit{Drug-target} (BindingDB, ChEMBL, DrugCentral, Guide to Pharmacology, STITCH), \textit{Metabolism (GEMs)} (MetAtlas, Recon3D), \textit{Subcellular localization} (Gene Ontology cellular-component subtree, UniProt, Reactome) and \textit{Literature references} (most resources) are curator-declared. The override list is recorded in the artifact's provenance sidecar under \texttt{parameters.expert\_overrides}.}
