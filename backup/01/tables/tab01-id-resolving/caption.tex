\textbf{Table 1: Identifier resolving across the integrated OmniPath Metabo resources.}\space
\figpanel{a}{Distinct identifiers contributed by each integrated resource on \texttt{dev3}, split by identifier type. The ten most-populated identifier types are shown as named columns; the long tail collapses into a single \texttt{Misc} column. The \texttt{Total} column is the row sum across all identifier types. The grand-summary row at the bottom of the body is the column sum across resources. Counts come from \texttt{identifier\_evidence} joined to \texttt{vocab\_identifier\_type} and \texttt{entity\_evidence\_identifier}; resources are sorted by \texttt{Total} descending.}
