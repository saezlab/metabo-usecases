\textbf{Table 3: Resource coverage of the manuscript record types.}\space
\figpanel{a}{Coverage of 19 manuscript record types across the integrated OmniPath Metabo resources. A cell is checked when the per-resource count of items in that record type is at least \texttt{N} (default \texttt{N=1}; the actual threshold is carried into the provenance sidecar's \texttt{parameters.threshold}). Rows are mapped to combinations of the cycle-001 \texttt{facet\_entity\_bitmap} (\texttt{entity\_type}, \texttt{chemical\_class}, \texttt{ontology\_id}, \texttt{structural\_specificity}) and \texttt{facet\_relation\_bitmap} (\texttt{predicate}); the Structures row is the only one that routes to \texttt{dev4} (the \texttt{structural\_specificity} facet bitmap is dev4-only). Rows whose underlying vocabulary has not yet landed in the cycle-001 build — \textit{Xenobiotics} (\texttt{chemical\_class.xenobiotic} deferred), \textit{Allosteric regulation}, \textit{Ligand-receptor}, \textit{Drug-target}, \textit{TF-target} (predicate vocabulary collapses into \texttt{Other} at the cycle-001 vocabulary; see FR-007d), \textit{Subcellular localization} (no distinct facet yet), \textit{Metabolism (GEMs)} (no GEM facet yet), \textit{Literature references} (no \texttt{facet\_evidence\_bitmap} yet) — render as empty rows rather than be synthesised from secondary tables.}
