\textbf{Figure 4: Comparison of old and new COSMOS prior-knowledge networks.}
The OmniPath Metabo database provides a substantially expanded prior-knowledge
network (PKN) for COSMOS relative to the legacy cosmosR PKN (v1.18.1).
\textbf{(a)} Schematic of PKN construction: the signed, directed OmniPath
Metabo network is collapsed to a binary network encoding activating (+1) and
inhibitory (\textminus{}1) edges across six interaction-type categories.
\textbf{(b)} Absolute edge counts by interaction-type category for the old PKN
(grey) and the new OmniPath Metabo PKN (colour-coded); the new PKN adds
coverage across signalling, transport, and ligand-receptor categories absent in
the legacy resource.
\textbf{(c)} Proportional composition of each PKN by category; the old PKN is
dominated by metabolic reactions while the new PKN distributes across a broader
mechanistic repertoire, enabling more comprehensive causal reasoning in COSMOS.
\textbf{(d)} Interaction-type legend.
\textbf{(e)} Simplified MOON activity-inference diagram: signed PKN edges
propagate observed metabolomics and transcriptomics constraints to infer
upstream node activities.
