\textbf{Figure 4: COSMOS+ prior-knowledge network --- scope extension, compartment coverage, resource contributions, and comparison with MetaLinksDB~2.0.}
The OmniPath Metabo COSMOS+ PKN substantially extends the legacy cosmosR PKN (v1.18.1) in both species coverage and interaction-type repertoire.
\textbf{(a)} Edge counts per interaction-type category for the old COSMOS PKN (human, species-unspecified) and COSMOS+ (human and mouse separately); categories present in COSMOS+ but absent from the old PKN appear with an empty baseline bar, making the scope extension explicit.
\textbf{(b)} COSMOS+ interactions tallied per annotated subcellular compartment; the \textit{locations} field encodes the compartment(s) associated with each edge --- if the field is unpopulated at build time, a single ``unannotated'' bar is shown as a build-state placeholder.
\textbf{(c)} Unique metabolite (ChEBI), protein (UniProt), and interaction counts contributed by each resource to the COSMOS+ network, illustrating the relative contribution and entity-coverage footprint of each data source.
\textbf{(d)} Side-by-side comparison of MetaLinksDB~2.0 (from the OmniPath Metabo Postgres deployment) and COSMOS+ by interaction type, highlighting the complementarity of the two resources.
\textbf{(S1)} Schematic of PKN construction: the signed, directed OmniPath Metabo network is collapsed to a binary network encoding activating (+1) and inhibitory (\textminus{}1) edges across interaction-type categories.
\textbf{(S2)} Regulation-type legend: visual key for the signed interaction categories used across the PKN.
\textbf{(S3)} Simplified MOON activity-inference diagram: signed PKN edges propagate observed metabolomics and transcriptomics constraints to infer upstream node activities via recursive scoring.
