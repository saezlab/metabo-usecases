\textbf{Figure 5: COSMOS+ prior-knowledge network --- regulation-type schematic, scope comparison, compartment coverage, and resource contributions.}
The OmniPath Metabo COSMOS+ PKN integrates signed, directed interactions of multiple regulation categories across subcellular compartments and upstream resources.
\textbf{(a)} Regulation-type legend: visual key for the signed interaction categories the COSMOS+ PKN carries (activating $+1$ vs.\ inhibitory $-1$ edges across the canonical interaction-type vocabulary).
\textbf{(b)} Species-aware comparison of the old COSMOS PKN against COSMOS+ by interaction type, with three bars per group (old COSMOS, human; COSMOS+ human; COSMOS+ mouse). Categories present only in COSMOS+ (receptors, inhibitors, allosteric regulators, transport, etc.) appear with an empty old-PKN bar, making the scope extension explicit; COSMOS+ \textit{catalysis} interactions (including the KEGG catalysis edges) are folded into the metabolic-reactions group so they align with the old PKN's metabolic-reactions bar.
\textbf{(c)} COSMOS+ interactions per annotated subcellular compartment, stacked by interaction type (metabolic reaction, transport, gene regulation, signaling, allosteric regulation, ligand receptor). Compartment single-letter codes are expanded to full names and child compartments are folded into their parent (extracellular/TCDB into Cell membrane, intermembrane space into Mitochondria). If the \textit{locations} field is unpopulated at build time, a single ``Unannotated'' bar is shown as a build-state placeholder.
\textbf{(d)} Unique metabolite (ChEBI) and protein (UniProt) entity counts contributed by the top-15 resources to the COSMOS+ network, drawn as grouped bars (Metabolites in teal and Proteins in magenta, side by side per resource); remaining resources are collapsed into ``Other''. Resource aliases are canonicalised so each resource appears once (hGEM\,=\,Human-GEM, mGEM\,=\,Mouse-GEM, R3D\,=\,Recon3D GEM, hGEM-T\,=\,Human-GEM transporter, MRCLinks\,=\,MRCLinksDB); the mouse GEM \textit{iMM1415} and \textit{mGEM} are kept as separate rows.
