\textbf{Figure 5: COSMOS+ prior-knowledge network --- compartment coverage and resource contributions.}
The OmniPath Metabo COSMOS+ PKN spans multiple subcellular compartments and integrates interactions from diverse metabolic and signalling resources.
\textbf{(a)} COSMOS+ interactions per annotated subcellular compartment, stacked by interaction type (metabolic reaction, transport, gene regulation, signaling, allosteric regulation, ligand receptor). Compartment single-letter codes are expanded to full names (Cytoplasm, Extracellular, Mitochondria, etc.\ per RECON/BiGG convention). If the \textit{locations} field is unpopulated at build time, a single ``Unannotated'' bar is shown as a build-state placeholder.
\textbf{(b)} Unique metabolite (ChEBI) and protein (UniProt) entity counts contributed by the top-15 resources to the COSMOS+ network (stacked bars); remaining resources are collapsed into ``Other''. Resource names are abbreviated (hGEM\,=\,Human-GEM, mGEM\,=\,Mouse-GEM, R3D\,=\,Recon3D GEM, hGEM-T\,=\,Human-GEM transporter, MRCLinks\,=\,MRCLinksDB).
\textbf{(S1)} Schematic of PKN construction: the signed, directed OmniPath Metabo network is collapsed to a binary network encoding activating (+1) and inhibitory (\textminus{}1) edges across interaction-type categories.
\textbf{(S2)} Regulation-type legend: visual key for the signed interaction categories used across the PKN.
\textbf{(S3)} Simplified MOON activity-inference diagram: signed PKN edges propagate observed metabolomics and transcriptomics constraints to infer upstream node activities via recursive scoring.
