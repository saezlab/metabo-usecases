\textbf{Figure 4: COSMOS+ prior-knowledge network --- scope extension, compartment coverage, resource contributions, and comparison with MetaLinksDB~2.0.}
The OmniPath Metabo COSMOS+ PKN substantially extends the legacy cosmosR PKN (v1.18.1) in both species coverage and interaction-type repertoire.
\textbf{(a)} Edge counts per interaction-type category for the old COSMOS PKN (human, species-unspecified) and COSMOS+ (human and mouse separately); categories present in COSMOS+ but absent from the old PKN appear with an empty baseline bar, making the scope extension explicit.
\textbf{(b)} COSMOS+ interactions per annotated subcellular compartment, stacked by interaction type (metabolic reaction, transport, gene regulation, signaling, allosteric regulation, ligand receptor). Compartment single-letter codes are expanded to full names (Cytoplasm, Extracellular, Mitochondria, etc.\ per RECON/BiGG convention). If the \textit{locations} field is unpopulated at build time, a single ``Unannotated'' bar is shown as a build-state placeholder.
\textbf{(c)} Unique metabolite (ChEBI) and protein (UniProt) entity counts contributed by the top-15 resources to the COSMOS+ network (stacked bars); remaining resources are collapsed into ``Other''. Resource names are abbreviated (hGEM\,=\,Human-GEM, mGEM\,=\,Mouse-GEM, R3D\,=\,Recon3D GEM, hGEM-T\,=\,Human-GEM transporter, MRCLinks\,=\,MRCLinksDB).
\textbf{(d)} Grouped-bar comparison of MetaLinksDB~2.0 and COSMOS+ by canonical interaction category (Transport, Ligand receptor, Catalysis, Gene regulation, Allosteric regulation). MetaLinksDB protein types are derived from Guide to Pharmacology (GtP) functional class annotations; the Allosteric regulation bar is COSMOS+-only, reflecting categories absent from the GtP classification.
\textbf{(S1)} Schematic of PKN construction: the signed, directed OmniPath Metabo network is collapsed to a binary network encoding activating (+1) and inhibitory (\textminus{}1) edges across interaction-type categories.
\textbf{(S2)} Regulation-type legend: visual key for the signed interaction categories used across the PKN.
\textbf{(S3)} Simplified MOON activity-inference diagram: signed PKN edges propagate observed metabolomics and transcriptomics constraints to infer upstream node activities via recursive scoring.
