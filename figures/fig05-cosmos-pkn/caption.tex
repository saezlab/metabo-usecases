\textbf{Figure 5: COSMOS+ prior-knowledge network --- regulation-type schematic, compartment coverage, and resource contributions.}
The OmniPath Metabo COSMOS+ PKN integrates signed, directed interactions of multiple regulation categories across subcellular compartments and upstream resources.
\textbf{(a)} Regulation-type legend: visual key for the signed interaction categories the COSMOS+ PKN carries (activating $+1$ vs.\ inhibitory $-1$ edges across the canonical interaction-type vocabulary).
\textbf{(b)} COSMOS+ interactions per annotated subcellular compartment, stacked by interaction type (metabolic reaction, transport, gene regulation, signaling, allosteric regulation, ligand receptor). Compartment single-letter codes are expanded to full names (Cytoplasm, Extracellular, Mitochondria, etc.\ per RECON/BiGG convention). If the \textit{locations} field is unpopulated at build time, a single ``Unannotated'' bar is shown as a build-state placeholder.
\textbf{(c)} Unique metabolite (ChEBI) and protein (UniProt) entity counts contributed by the top-15 resources to the COSMOS+ network, drawn as grouped bars (Metabolites in teal and Proteins in magenta, side by side per resource); remaining resources are collapsed into ``Other''. Resource names are abbreviated (hGEM\,=\,Human-GEM, mGEM\,=\,Mouse-GEM, R3D\,=\,Recon3D GEM, hGEM-T\,=\,Human-GEM transporter, MRCLinks\,=\,MRCLinksDB).
