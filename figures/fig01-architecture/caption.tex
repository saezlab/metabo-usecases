\textbf{Figure 1: Architecture of the OmniPath ecosystem.} Workflow diagram of
the OmniPath Metabo database build and serving stack — original resources at
the top, download and processing in \texttt{omnipath-resources} (\texttt{pypath}),
three parallel database builds in the middle band
(\texttt{omnipath-utils} \textbar{} \texttt{omnipath-build} \textbar{} \texttt{omnipath-metabo})
each backed by its own PostgreSQL instance, three matching web APIs
(\texttt{utils.omnipathdb.org} \textbar{} \texttt{omnipathdb.org} \textbar{} \texttt{metabo.omnipathdb.org}),
and the R \textbar{} Python clients at the bottom. Headline counts shown on
the diagram (entities, metabolite-protein interactions, structures,
annotations) are sourced from the FR-043 statistics digest at
\texttt{figures/fig01-architecture/panel-a-stats/} and are pinned to the
snapshot identifier recorded in the figure's provenance sidecar.
