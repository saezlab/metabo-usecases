\textbf{Figure 6: Cancer cell lines use case --- OmniPath Metabo evidence for azelate.}
Evidence retrieval from the OmniPath Metabo database for azelate, a metabolite from the cancer cell lines use case.
\textbf{(a)} Relation counts by interaction type, stacked by upstream resource (ChEMBL, DrugCentral, FooDB, HMDB, MACDB, MetAtlas, PFOCR, RHEA, STITCH, ChEBI, SwissLipids). Data are restricted to the resolved canonical \emph{Azelate} entity (InChI key \texttt{BDJRBEYXGGNYIS-UHFFFAOYSA-N}); the two source-specific seed records are retained in the supplementary tables (\textit{Azelaic acid (PTF52203)}, \textit{Azelaic acid; nonanedioic acid}) but excluded from the plotted counts.
\textbf{(b)} Cancer-association evidence for the resolved \emph{Azelate} entity: evidence-record counts per disease type stacked by sample (tissue) type (Blood, Serum, Plasma, Tissue, Stool, Urine, Sweat, Cells). Counts are direct MACDB extractions through the OmniPath Metabo FastAPI surface.
