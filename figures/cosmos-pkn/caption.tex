\textbf{Figure 5: COSMOS+ prior-knowledge network --- regulation-type schematic, compartment coverage, resource contributions, and scope comparison.}
The OmniPath Metabo COSMOS+ PKN integrates signed, directed interactions of multiple regulation categories across subcellular compartments and upstream resources.
\textbf{(a)} Regulation-type legend: visual key for the signed interaction categories the COSMOS+ PKN carries (activating $+1$ vs.\ inhibitory $-1$ edges across the canonical interaction-type vocabulary).
\textbf{(b)} COSMOS+ interactions per annotated subcellular compartment, stacked by interaction type. The legend uses short labels whose full forms are: metabolic reaction (enzyme--metabolite), transport (transporter--metabolite), gene regulation (TF--target, GRN), signaling (PPI), allosteric regulation (metabolite--enzyme), and ligand receptor (receptor--metabolite). Compartment single-letter codes are expanded to full names and child compartments are folded into their parent (extracellular/TCDB into Cell membrane, intermembrane space into Mitochondria). If the \textit{locations} field is unpopulated at build time, a single ``Unannotated'' bar is shown as a build-state placeholder.
\textbf{(c)} Unique metabolite (ChEBI) and protein (UniProt) entity counts contributed by the top-15 resources to the COSMOS+ network, drawn as grouped bars (Metabolites in teal and Proteins in magenta, side by side per resource); remaining resources are collapsed into ``Other''. Resource aliases are canonicalised so each resource appears once (hGEM\,=\,Human-GEM, mGEM\,=\,Mouse-GEM, R3D\,=\,Recon3D GEM, hGEM-T\,=\,Human-GEM transporter, MRCLinks\,=\,MRCLinksDB); the mouse GEM \textit{iMM1415} and \textit{mGEM} are kept as separate rows.
\textbf{(d)} Old COSMOS vs.\ COSMOS+ compared in three facets, one per interaction-type category distinguishable in the old COSMOS PKN --- protein interactions, metabolite interactions, and metabolic reactions. Bars are grouped on the y-axis and coloured \emph{only} by organism (human, mouse). The top group of each facet is the old COSMOS PKN as a single human bar; the remaining groups are the COSMOS+ interaction types, most carrying both a human and a mouse bar. Protein interactions: old COSMOS, Signaling (PPI), and GRN --- the COSMOS+ protein PKN is sourced from OmniPath and is human-only, so those two groups are single human bars (edge counts from \texttt{build\_ppi()} / \texttt{build\_grn()}). Metabolite interactions: old COSMOS (STITCH), Transporter, Allosteric regulation (BRENDA), Receptor. Metabolic reactions: old COSMOS (Recon3D) vs.\ new COSMOS (Recon3D + KEGG + GEM).
